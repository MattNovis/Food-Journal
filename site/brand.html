Rules

Do

  • Let photos run big, and never filter them.
  • Keep it simple: colour, type and photos do the work.
  • Write the ledger as a sentence, in the traveller's voice.
  • Use soft, offset, green-tinted shadows.

Don't

  • Use gradient text, glassy cards, or labels stacked above headings.
  • Add decorative motifs (leaves, fronds, patterns) or emoji as icons.
  • Use hard block shadows or glows.
  • Add a colour without adding it to brand.css and DESIGN.md.
